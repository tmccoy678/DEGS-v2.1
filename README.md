# DEGS

DEGS—the Dobeworks Engineering Governance System—is a tailored local framework for recording engineering intent, risk, authority, evidence, recovery, verification, validation, review, and phase handoff. Its deterministic, read-only engineering gate evaluates structured task records against the selected local risk tier and returns decisions and diagnostics; it does not execute tasks, establish that evidence is true, or grant approval.

Its control model is a tailored local synthesis of systems and software engineering standards and handbooks, secure-development and software-supply-chain guidance, foundational engineering literature, and bounded public industry practices. Those sources inform local controls without transferring their applicability, endorsement, certification, or compliance.
