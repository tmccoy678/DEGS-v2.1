## Traceability boundaries

- `CURRENT REQUIREMENT` sources are requirements only within their own stated applicability. Their DEGS mappings are local adaptations.
- `FINAL STANDARD` means the source itself is published or approved; it does not mean DEGS conforms to it.
- `DRAFT` sources are advisory only. `DEGS-SRC-NIST-SSDF12-DRAFT` creates no DEGS obligation.
- `FOUNDATIONAL` sources support derived design principles, not modern security or lifecycle compliance.
- `PUBLIC INDUSTRY PRACTICE` sources are informative only and are not used as normative support.
- Enforcement references identify the intended control surface. The executable gate evaluates submitted evidence; it does not execute work or prove that an evidence file is authentic.
